Production notes

Capture

  • Record at 1920×1080 or larger. The deck is authored at 1080p, so anything smaller resamples the type and it shows.
  • Hide your browser chrome — full screen for the deck, and hide bookmarks for the site. A visible tab bar makes a product look like a prototype.
  • Use a clean browser profile. No extension icons, no notifications mid-take.

Pacing

  • Let every animation finish. The charts are the argument; a half-drawn one is worse than none.
  • Scroll with the wheel or arrow keys at a steady rate. Do not accelerate.

Audio

  • Record the voiceover separately, indoors, close to the mic. Do not try to capture narration while training.
  • Leave the app's own audio in under the voiceover on the rep beats — the count and the haptic click sell it.

Edit

  • No speed ramps. The charts read as data; sped up they read as decoration.
  • Hold the fatigue curve. It is the one image in the video that no other team has.
  • End on black with the wordmark and the URL. No music sting over the final line.
If you are forced under 90 seconds Keep beats 1, 2, 3, 4 and the sign-off. Cut the coach, privacy, multiplayer and beyond-the-game beats entirely rather than shortening all of them — four beats told properly beats nine beats rushed. The fatigue curve stays in every version.
