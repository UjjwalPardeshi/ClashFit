Why this exists

A tracker can't see you. A fitness game doesn't care whether you did it right.

Three failures. Everyone in this category has at least one.

01 · Inert

Logging isn't motivation

A workout goes in, a graph comes out, and nothing pulls you back tomorrow.

02 · Blind

Movement isn't effort

Motion games read a wave or a jump. A bad squat and a good squat score the same.

03 · Exposed

The footage leaves the room

Almost every competitor sends your workout footage to a server. That is why most people never try one.

The loop

Reps in. Damage out.

One still second calibrates you. The first rep sets your baseline, so everything after is scored against you.

01

Prop the phone

Side-on, whole body in frame. A rep the camera could not see is never counted.

02

Every rep lands a hit

Four sub-scores grade every rep. The hit lands inside 100 ms, with a sound — so it works face-down.

03

It reads the fade

Your output decays as you tire. The boss reads the decay and answers it.

The part nobody else has

It knows when you're gassed.

Not a difficulty slider. Not a self-reported effort rating. Three measured quantities, computed per rep on the phone, against your own first three reps.

  • Velocity lossHow much slower the lifting half of the rep has become. This is the same signal velocity-based training has used for decades.
  • ROM collapseRange of motion shrinking rep over rep — the depth quietly going away before you notice it has.
  • Pause growthThe gap between reps stretching out. The most human tell there is.

And it never punishes you for it. The boss staggers as you fade, and the mercy rule guarantees you an ending.

Circuit

One prescribed sequence across four movement families, everyone moving through it together.

Cross-family

Community that survives a dead signal

  • Ghosts are files. Send a set through any app you already use.
  • Challenge cards. A run compresses to a ~300-character code. No link, no login.
  • Crew ledgers. Phones merge records whenever they are in the same room.

The maths, in full

  • bossHp = maxHp − Σ damage over the de-duplicated union of every player's events.
  • Idempotent keys — duplicates change nothing, drops heal on the next tail.
  • Every phone computes the boss itself. Nobody desyncs, and nobody's exit ends the fight.

One fatigue model, five families

  • Reps fade as velocity loss and shrinking range.
  • Holds fade as tremor and angular drift.
  • Asanas fade as accuracy drift across the hold.
  • Cardio fades as cadence and amplitude decay.
  • Jumps fade as height decay and stiffening landings — which is where injuries actually happen.

Ladders, not an easy mode

  • Chair squats and wall push-ups are first-class rungs, never an apology.
  • Every exercise is a config record, so the library grows without a release.
  • Circuit walks one sequence across four families in a session.

The coach

The coach and the villain are the same model.

Eleven numbers go to a language model on the phone's own neural processor. It writes the coaching and the taunt — and never sees your video.

Spoken

It talks, so it works face-down

Every verdict has a sound and every line is spoken. The whole app works with the screen off.

Grounded

It only cites real numbers

A validator checks every line against the telemetry. Invented numbers are dropped, never spoken.

Local

Your language, on your phone

A local model means Indic-language coaching is a model swap, not a rewrite.

Everything else

Health is a set of interacting resources. So is a character.

Exercise is damage. Sleep is your energy pool. Breathing is a shield. Every health behaviour moves one bar.

Power Strength quality
 Base damage per rep.
Stamina Cardio and runs
 How long before the fatigue bands escalate.
Focus Breathing sessions
 Reduces the boss's critical damage; steadies the tremor threshold in holds.
Mobility Yoga accuracy, range tests
 Unlocks dodges and higher rungs. You shouldn't load a movement you can't reach the bottom of.
Energy Sleep duration and regularity
 Your daily action pool. A badly slept day offers shorter, gentler fights — never a red screen.
Nourishment Meals photographed on-device
 Temporary buffs. Patterns and trends — never a calorie number presented as fact.
Resilience Consistency and recovery days
 Softens the cost of a missed day. Taking a rest day grows it.

Staying

Streaks that survive real life.

The most common way a fitness app loses someone is a punitive streak. So ClashFit doesn't have one.

Protected

One rest day a week

Automatic, not something you have to remember to claim. Rest is training.

Positive

Rest days score

Taking one grows RESILIENCE. The most contrarian decision in the design, and the correct one.

Kind

No number you lost

A broken streak says “back at it”. It never says “you lost 41 days”, because that sentence closes apps.

Finite

Seasons that end

Thirty-odd nodes over six to eight weeks, then a reset with your stats intact.

Privacy

No cloud box on this diagram.

Pose, form scoring, the fatigue model and the language model all run on the phone. No account, no upload, nothing to breach.

Not a policy we could change in an update — the shipping app requests no internet permission at all. You can check.
